# ADR-0001 — Architecture change procedure

**Status:** accepted.

## Context

Stormwatch is expanding beyond its MVP. Architecture changes should produce a
measurable improvement while preserving deterministic gameplay, accepted art and
content contracts, and the working deployment. A plausible code-level bottleneck
alone does not prove a player benefit.

## Decision

For each architecture job:

1. Read the scope, architecture, glossary and relevant accepted decisions. State the
   observed problem, the proposed module seam, and the behavior that must survive.
2. Capture a reproducible before measurement for the affected path. Record the
   environment and limitations, including when a browser simulation stands in for
   unavailable physical hardware.
3. Choose a small interface that owns the relevant policy and lifetime. Record a
   consequential choice in `docs/decisions` with context, decision, consequences
   and verification.
4. Implement the change and run focused behavior tests, `npm run check`,
   `npm test`, and `npm run build`. Verify authored content through the promotion
   round trip when content or loading contracts change.
5. Repeat the same measurement after the change. Report raw evidence, median and
   p95, regressions as well as gains, and the practical limit of the conclusion.
6. Put a repeatable regression check in CI where the measurement is stable enough
   to gate a PR. After merge, verify the main workflow and Pages deployment.

An architecture job is complete when the intended benefit is measured, behavior
checks pass, the decision and evidence are recorded, and deployment is healthy.
Product direction, acceptance budget changes and new asset spending still require
owner approval.

## Consequences

Architecture work carries a before/after comparison and a decision record. CI
thresholds may allow measured runner variance and normal content growth; the
threshold and its rationale belong with the benchmark procedure. Simulated browser
results are reported as simulated results.

## Verification

The battle art loading job is the first application of this procedure. Its
[benchmark procedure](benchmarks/art-loading.md), before/after evidence and
associated decision record show whether every step was applied.
