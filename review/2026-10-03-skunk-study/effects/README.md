## Scope

This is a scripted visual comparison, not a Game attempt. Existing committed
Rat, Weasel and Boar parts are assembled directly from their descriptors at rest
and translated for inspection. No enemy walking or combat logic is being authored.
Labels explain illustrative outcomes; they are not approved combat-text designs.
The staging lanes are not proposed encounter geometry.

The comparison places an impact at .8 seconds, retires its stationary burst
after .85 seconds, and attaches gas to three example Rats until 4.8 seconds.
An illustrative evading Weasel, immune Boar and later Rat remain untreated.
These numbers and outcomes are demonstration inputs, not delegated tuning or
proof of hit eligibility. Poison lifecycle and state/event interfaces remain
owned by the combat work. There is no damaging or persistent ground cloud.

All new effects are procedural Canvas drawing: radial puffs and curved wisps,
with deterministic phase offsets. No raster art was generated for this stage.
Body art provenance remains in each original `public/art/v2/*/rig.json`.
