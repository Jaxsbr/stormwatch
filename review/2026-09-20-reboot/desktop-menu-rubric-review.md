## Three highest-impact fixes

1. Fit briefing into1280x720 without scrolling: shorten cards, reduce decorative gaps and preserve the primary action in the visible frame. All four advantage choices and selected state should be comparable simultaneously.
2. Carry the authored timber/brass language into primary interactive controls, especially advantage cards and build dock. Use a consistent border/corner treatment and a clearly different selected state; avoid simply increasing gold strokes. The current pause frame is a useful visual reference.
3. Reduce map destination plaques and place them below or beside landmarks. Current large opaque plaques cover much of the standing-stone clearing, bridge and connecting path. Use a compact marker/title with stars and show extra action wording on selection, allowing the map to do visual storytelling.
