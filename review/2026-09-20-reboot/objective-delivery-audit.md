## Completion interpretation and limits

Each original objective is supported by the linked implementation and retained reviews. Scores improved from 1–2/5 in the baseline to supported 4/5 across all nine dimensions. The committed source reproduces the measured build without ignored artwork or generation access. The actual 50-second demo and its source captures are delivered.

This is convincing demo scope, not a claim of universal device performance, perfect animation, broad strategy balance or a polished promotional trailer. Chrome 153 retains isolated stress stalls despite passing the p95 target; the original in-app Chromium 152 desktop profile passes all three runs. Physical-device performance and audio listening remain unverified. Continuous audiovisual playback was not independently assessed; consecutive normal-play frames, actual recordings, live interaction and scoped timing provide the stated evidence. No push or publication is included.
