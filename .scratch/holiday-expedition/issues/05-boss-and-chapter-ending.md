## Owner-approved replan — 26 September 2026

The first five recipes in `../wave-plan.md` are too light for the intended third-map challenge. Rainstone's accepted fourth wave sends 28 Rats and 60 Weasels in twelve 6–8-enemy bursts, with 0.2-second spawn spacing, five seconds between bursts, 1.1× Weasel movement and two seconds of evasion every five. Last Lantern's current waves 4 and 5 send 22 and 24 enemies respectively, with arrivals spread across 2.5–3 second gaps; wave 5 also slows Weasels to 0.9×. That does not yet provide the close, repeated placement pressure the final map needs.

Keep the six-wave teaching arc, but use deliberate packets and shorter recovery windows. The owner approved these recipes before implementation. Initial gold was a tuning hypothesis; seed-42 candidate evidence supports 235 starting gold, while 120- and 180-gold versions failed. Kill reward and clear rewards remain as authored.

The scheduler processes groups sequentially and each group's `gap` advances the timeline by a spawn interval, so author packets as ordinary groups with their intended delays; do not use `batchSize` in a way that makes a following group begin before the packet has spawned. Preserve the five-second and four-second rests measured from the prior packet's last enemy to the next packet's first enemy. Verify the rally recipients by path distance in the deterministic Game scenario.

The approved recipes are implemented in `src/content/the-last-lantern.ts` and the active Map 3 plan is in `../wave-plan.md`. ## Owner-requested boss retune and copy removal — 26 September 2026

After playtesting, the owner reported wave 6 was too easy relative to wave 5: the two escort parties worked, but the towers defeated the Roadwarden easily. The owner requested double boss health and five escort parties total. Boss health is now 2200; after the 8-Rat vanguard and Roadwarden, the five escort parties spawn at boss ages 4.5s, 14.5s, 24.5s, 34.5s and 44.5s, in 7-Weasel / 5-Rat / 7-Weasel / 5-Rat / 7-Weasel order.

The owner also asked to remove the escort-call notification, the defeat directive beside the wave control, and the boxed boss explanation from the Last Lantern briefing. Boss arrival/rally text toasts and the briefing/battle directives are removed. The boss health panel, visual rally tell, audio cue, and required-defeat simulation rule remain.

At seed 42, the two earlier successful build lines and the poor-placement line all lose at wave 6 before killing the Roadwarden. With the final 7-Weasel / 5-Rat parties, the upgraded-Squirrel line loses to boss escape with one life remaining; the other two run out of lives. Exact results are recorded in `../wave-plan.md`; treat them as pressure evidence, not proof that the new finale is balanced or completable for a new player.
