# Make both encounters tactically distinct

Status: needs-info

Depends on: 06-campaign-challenge-brainstorm.md

## Problem

The expedition map already links Lantern Pass and Rainstone Crossing, and Rainstone has a different trail. Both encounters still use the same four enemy roles and broadly similar tower answers. The owner has agreed that Stormwatch should combine its fixed-route expedition with replayable, learnable enemy waves. Translate that direction into encounter purposes after the unresolved wave-learning decisions are settled in [issue 06](06-campaign-challenge-brainstorm.md).

## Direction

- Make route and terrain differences alter where strong placements exist and how long towers cover the trail.
- Introduce an enemy behavior in a readable, low-risk way, then revisit it and combine it with learned enemies; specify the progression after issue 06 is resolved.
- Change the defense problem through enemy behaviors and tower/enemy matchups; do not use the retired numeric worksheet as evidence.
- Let short, visual story beats support the woodland journey; avoid relying on long exposition or a permanent power grind.
- Keep each map understandable through silhouettes, path cues, and concise titles.

## Acceptance

- The direction chosen in [issue 06](06-campaign-challenge-brainstorm.md) is translated into distinct encounter purposes.
- A side-by-side comparison names the placement decision and enemy pressure that distinguish each encounter.
- Differences are visible in route/terrain and/or enemy behavior, rather than only health, speed, count, or wave length.
- The current meaningful wave-end economy and no-required-grinding progress remain viable.
- Added content stays data-driven and deterministic. Any new enemy rule has focused rule tests before production; no new asset purchase is presumed.
